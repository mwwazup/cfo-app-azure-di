## Reading the Metrics

### Performance Snapshot Cards

**Current Month Revenue**
- Shows total revenue for selected month
- Green ↗ = Revenue increased
- Red ↘ = Revenue decreased
- Percentage shows how much change

**Appointments**
- Total jobs completed
- Growth % vs comparison period
- Indicates volume trends

**Avg Ticket**
- Revenue per appointment
- Shows pricing power
- Higher = better revenue efficiency

**YTD Growth**
- Year-to-date growth %
- Always compares to previous year
- Shows overall business trajectory

### Service Performance Table

**Revenue Column**
- Total revenue per service
- Sorted highest to lowest

**Growth Column**
- % change vs comparison period
- Green = growing
- Red = declining

**Appointments Column**
- Number of jobs per service
- Shows volume by service type

**Appt Growth Column**
- % change in appointment volume
- Identifies demand trends

**Avg Ticket Column**
- Revenue per appointment by service
- Shows which services are most valuable

## Common Questions

### "Revenue is down this month - is that bad?"
**Check the comparison mode:**
- **Month-over-Month**: Could be seasonal (normal)
- **Year-over-Year**: If down, investigate why

**Look at YTD Growth:**
- If YTD is positive, you're still growing overall
- One month doesn't make a trend

### "Which service should I focus on?"
**Look for:**
1. **Highest revenue** = Your bread and butter
2. **Highest growth %** = What's working
3. **Highest avg ticket** = Most profitable per job

**Your answer (Oct 2025):**
- Focus: Window Cleaning (Residential) - 35% of revenue
- Grow: Holiday Lighting - 245% growth
- Optimize: Gutter Cleaning - Only 7% of revenue (upsell?)

### "How do I know if I'm improving?"
**Check these metrics:**
1. **YTD Growth**: Should be positive
2. **Avg Ticket**: Should be increasing (pricing power)
3. **Revenue per Service**: Top services should be growing

### Monthly Review Routine
1. **First week of month**: Check previous month performance
2. **Set to MoM**: See immediate trends
3. **Switch to YoY**: Confirm long-term growth

2. **Identify patterns**: Which months are strong/weak?

5. **Track progress**: Monthly check-ins

1. **High revenue + high growth** = Double down
